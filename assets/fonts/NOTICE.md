# Small Slant

`smslant.flf` is the unmodified SmSlant/Small Slant FIGlet font by Glenn Chappell,
June 1993, based on Small and Slant, distributed with FIGlet release 2.1
(12 August 1994), with Paul Burton's December 1996 layout-parameter update.
The embedded comments retain the attribution and permission statement.

Source: [FIGlet font at commit 202a0a8110650a943f1125f536b3bb455cf72ee1](https://github.com/cmatsuoka/figlet/blob/202a0a8110650a943f1125f536b3bb455cf72ee1/fonts/smslant.flf).
The [FIGlet distribution license](FIGlet-LICENSE) is retained alongside it.
Exact SHA-256 is recorded in [third-party inputs](../../conformance/third-party-inputs.json).
The Rust renderer is the Apache-2.0 `figlet-rs` 1.0.0 crate pinned in Cargo.lock.

The five compatibility goldens were supplied with their corresponding Craftguild
products. Two additional product-name goldens are generated with the same font.
Product source and build/runtime paths do not require the renderer or this font.
